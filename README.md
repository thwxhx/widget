## 📖 About

**VivoSports** is a sports streaming website that brings live and upcoming matches together on one clean page. Pick your sport, pick your day, tap a match and you're in.

Football fixtures from international friendlies, Nations League and local leagues, plus cricket, all updated regularly.

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🏆 **Sport Filters** | Switch between All, Football and Cricket |

## ❓ FAQ

**Is VivoSports free to watch?**
Yes, matches are free to access.

**Do you host the videos?**
No. VivoSports only links to third-party pages and does not host any video content.

**A match isn't showing. What should I do?**
Matches are added shortly before kickoff. Check our Telegram or WhatsApp for the latest updates.
